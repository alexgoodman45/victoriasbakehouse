About Our Bakery

Victoria’s Bakehouse offers an impressive variety of cookies, including some of the most beautifully decorated you’ll ever see. In addition to cookies, the bakery provides a selection of drop cookies, gourmet treats, cupcakes, and fully decorated cakes. Whether you’re hosting a child’s birthday, a baby shower, a holiday celebration, or wedding, Victoria’s Bake House has you covered for all your baked goods needs.
